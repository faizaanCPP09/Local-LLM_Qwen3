# Sample IT Policy (test document - replace with your own files)

## Password policy
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email and VPN access.
Never share your password, including with IT staff.

## Requesting new software
Submit a request through the IT Service Desk portal with a business justification.
Your line manager must approve the request before IT installs the software.
Typical turnaround is 3 working days.

## Lost or stolen laptop
Report it to the IT Service Desk immediately, and within 1 hour at most.
IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. You will also need to file an incident report.

## VPN access
VPN is required when accessing internal systems from outside the office.
Install the approved VPN client from the Company Portal app and sign in with MFA.
